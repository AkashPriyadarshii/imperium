# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-01

First build. A private, on-device life ledger: log anything, see what you did, and keep it all on the phone. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

- **Ledger**: ten categories (food, water, health, study, fitness, sleep, mood, skin-hair, finance, freeform) plus a freeform catch-all. Every entry is text-first — an emoji, rating, or amount are optional.
- **Dashboard**: today's discipline grid, streak counter, weekly sleep/spend stats, habits.
- **Log**: capture fast. Backfill any past date/time. Repeat, edit, delete recent entries.
- **Stats**: monthly summary, streak math, 7/30-day trends, category totals, and an honest correlation probe — rule/math based, no fake AI.
- **Automation**: two daily reminder nudges (adaptive exact→inexact alarms) and a habit ledger with a weekly target.
- **Batch entry**: paste a JSON envelope (LLM output) to import many rows at once, with unknown-category mapping and per-row review.
- **Export / import**: full backup as JSON, merge-by-id on restore (no duplicates).
- **Biometric lock**: optional fingerprint/facial gate on launch (best effort).
- **Armored reset**: type RESET to confirm, backup written first.
- **Theme**: dark (default), light, or system.
- **History**: search every entry you've written, grouped by day.
- **Edge-to-edge**: Android 15/16 status/nav bars stay clear of the content.
